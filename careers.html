Simple & Transparent Hiring Process

No complex loops. We value your time as much as ours and keep our process straightforward.

01

Step 01

Application Submission

Submit your resume and portfolio. We review every application carefully within 48 hours.

02

Step 02

Initial Screening

A brief 15-minute introductory call to align on expectations, career goals, and core culture values.

03

Step 03

Discussion & Assessment

Hands-on assessment or technical walkthrough. Show us how you approach and solve real-world tasks.

04

Step 04

Final Interview

In-depth discussion with project leads and founders to detail team synergy, scope of work, and roadmap.

05

Step 05

Offer & Onboarding

Receive your offer, complete digital signing, and begin a structured week-long team introduction.
